# Proposed Feature - Analaytics

## Who
Users may be interested in collecting data from their shortened link, extending beyond just the amount of times a link was clicked.

## How

Cloudflare allows typical telemtry such as IP addresses and devices assigned from that IP.

## Why
Data is always a great thing to collect, and link shorteners are typically used by community managers.

In the app itself:

An entirely separate section on the dashboard should lead the user to a dedicated Analytics tab, visualizing countries and devices collected from each link itself. tHe option to choose analytics per link or in total is necessary, as well as selecting an arbitrary interval of time (with a minimum of the first link created by the user's timestamp).

For the Opus session:
Any suggestions to improve the core idea itself is welcome, but not necessary as the scope itself is small, and only needs to be proven to work and properly collect telemtry. Privacy is not a concern here (but definitely considered and would be accounted for on a live platform).
