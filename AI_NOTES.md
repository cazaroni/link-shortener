# My utilized AI tools

## Claude Code
The main and only tool utilized for developing the link shortener. Utilziing an Opus orchestrator to have its own context window perserved as much as possible is how I usually develop, choosing to keep this philosophy even if the scope of the project itself is small and would probably not force a compact.

The particular models used were Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5.

While I usually consider the first prompt sent to an agent as the most important one since it'll maintain the direction you want the most throughout the first couple of turns, the prompt I will contribute the most amount of praise to is allowing the orchestrator to propose any suggestions to the feature I added to the scope. I did not consider the referrer aspect to telemetry and is just as useful as the country and device portions gathered. 

A small mishap one of the agents had was letting go of the process serving the backend too soon. A simple inspection into the browser's dev tools allowed me to correct this hiccup and everything proceeded as normal.

No other AI tools were utilized during this session.
